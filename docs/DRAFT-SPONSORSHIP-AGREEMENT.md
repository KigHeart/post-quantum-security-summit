## 2. Event scope

The Organiser is convening a technical summit on quantum-resistant cryptography, datacentre security and critical infrastructure resilience. The main programme is proposed for 19–22 January 2027. A single pre-summit Night of Code is proposed for 18 January 2027. Dates, venue, format and programme remain subject to confirmation.

## 3. Payment schedule

Unless the parties complete a different schedule in the commercial schedule:

- 50% of the cash contribution is payable within 14 calendar days after execution and receipt of a valid invoice.
- 50% is payable no later than 30 calendar days before the event.
- No partner benefits dependent on payment need be activated until the corresponding amount is received.
- All payments must be made only to the verified account stated on an Organiser-issued invoice. Bank details must never be accepted solely from an email change request without independent verification.

## 4. Partner deliverables

The parties must complete a written schedule listing exact deliverables, quantities, placement, dimensions, deadlines, speaking or demonstration format, passes, exhibition requirements and any restrictions. Benefits are limited to those expressly written in that schedule. No guaranteed audience size, sales lead volume, media coverage, procurement outcome or regulatory endorsement is promised.

## 5. Brand and intellectual property

Each party retains ownership of its pre-existing names, marks, software, materials and intellectual property. A party may use the other's marks only for the event purpose, in the approved form and for the agreed period. Approval must be obtained before publication. No party may imply that the University of Nairobi or any public institution endorses a partner or product without separate written authority.

## 6. Programme independence and conduct

The Organiser retains editorial control of the technical programme. Sponsorship does not purchase research conclusions, speaker statements, standards positions, procurement preference or access to confidential delegate data. Partners and participants must comply with the event code of conduct, venue rules, challenge rules and applicable law.

## 7. Delegate information and privacy

The Organiser controls delegate registration and communications. Personal data will not be disclosed to a Partner merely because it sponsors the event. Any data sharing, lead retrieval or direct marketing requires a separate, specific, lawful arrangement and appropriate delegate notice/choice. Partners must not collect unnecessary sensitive personal information.

## 8. Cancellation, postponement and force majeure

The parties must select and complete a written policy covering cancellation, postponement, material programme changes, venue unavailability, force majeure, refunds, credit against a rescheduled event and treatment of costs already irreversibly incurred. No default refund promise is made by this template. Any agreed policy must comply with applicable law.

## 9. Records, tax and reconciliation

The Organiser will keep reasonable records of invoices, receipts and delivery of the agreed partner benefits. Each party is responsible for its own tax obligations. The invoice and final agreement must state applicable VAT, withholding tax and other statutory treatment after professional review.

## 10. Liability and dispute resolution

The parties must obtain legal review and complete provisions on insurance, indemnities, limitation of liability, governing law, dispute escalation and courts or arbitration before signature. Nothing in this template excludes liability where exclusion is prohibited by law.

## 11. Term and termination

This Agreement begins on the date of the last signature and ends when the agreed benefits, payments and close-out obligations are complete, unless terminated earlier under the completed commercial schedule. Any termination rights and consequences must be completed before execution.
