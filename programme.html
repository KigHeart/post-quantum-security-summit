SUMMIT PROGRAMME / 19—22 JANUARY 2027

Four days.
One engineering transition.

The programme below is a proposed technical framework to guide speaker invitations and session development. Session titles, speakers, timings and formats remain subject to confirmation.

PROGRAMME FRAMEWORK · NOT A FINAL RUN OF SHOW
DAY 01 19 JANUARY · TUESDAY

Understand the transition

Threat landscape, quantum risk, cryptographic dependencies and the strategic case for early migration.

  • Opening and summit objectives
  • Quantum computing: realistic threat horizons and uncertainty
  • Harvest-now, decrypt-later risk and data longevity
  • Post-quantum standards and migration landscape
  • Executive and institutional risk discussion
DAY 02 20 JANUARY · WEDNESDAY

Engineer cryptographic migration

Algorithms and protocols meet real systems, software estates, PKI, key management and hardware.

  • Post-quantum algorithms and implementation choices
  • Hybrid cryptography and protocol transition
  • Cryptographic inventory and dependency discovery
  • PKI, certificates, HSMs and key lifecycle management
  • Interoperability and performance considerations
DAY 03 21 JANUARY · THURSDAY

Secure the datacentre stack

Translate cryptographic change into operational controls across compute, network, storage and trust boundaries.

  • Datacentre security architecture and trust boundaries
  • Firmware, secure boot, identity and workload security
  • Cloud, virtualisation and network infrastructure
  • Testing, assurance and operational resilience
  • Migration planning workshop and engineering case studies
DAY 04 22 JANUARY · FRIDAY

Build the ecosystem

Coordinate standards, procurement, policy, skills and cross-sector implementation.

  • Regulatory and standards perspectives
  • Procurement requirements and supplier readiness
  • African capacity, research and skills development
  • Sector roundtables and implementation priorities
  • Closing synthesis and next-step commitments
