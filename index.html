01 / THE CHALLENGE

Cryptographic migration
is already an infrastructure concern.

The transition to post-quantum security is not a future-only research question. Long-lived data, cryptographic dependencies, hardware estates and complex supply chains make preparation a present engineering responsibility.

Datacentre operators and the organisations that depend on them need a practical way to discover cryptographic exposure, prioritise migration, validate implementations and coordinate change across infrastructure.

This summit creates a working forum for the people responsible for that transition: researchers, security engineers, infrastructure leaders, policymakers and technology providers.
