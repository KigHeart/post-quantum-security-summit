The management fee is a proposed fixed fee for convening, programme development, sponsor acquisition, production oversight and delivery management. The 50/50 surplus split is a negotiation proposal and requires a signed agreement defining the other beneficiary, eligible costs, reserves, tax treatment, audit rights and settlement date. Tilil's gross return is not net profit: its own internal costs, tax and any applicable withholding are not deducted here.

Financial governance: Do not commit expenditure against unsigned sponsorship pledges. Use a dedicated event ledger and bank/accounting controls; record every sponsor agreement, invoice, receipt, refund and supplier payment. Ring-fence contingency. Reforecast monthly and again at each major commitment gate. If revenue falls short, reduce variable scope before incurring unfunded liabilities.

04 / FUNDING SEQUENCE

Secure cash before scaling commitments.

  1. Anchor commitment: secure the strategic partner or equivalent institutional underwriting.
  2. Partner pipeline: convert gold, silver, bronze and exhibitor prospects into signed agreements and staged payments.
  3. Delegate sales: open paid registration only after venue, fees, refund terms, privacy and payment controls are approved.
  4. Supplier procurement: obtain at least comparable quotations and approve deposits against cash received.
  5. Close-out: reconcile the ledger, settle liabilities, calculate the verified surplus and distribute only after sign-off.
