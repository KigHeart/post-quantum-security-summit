How the summit organiser intends to handle delegate expressions of interest.

DRAFT — NOT YET APPROVED FOR LIVE COLLECTION. This notice is published for review while the registration service is being prepared. The organiser must confirm the legal controller identity, hosting location, recipients, retention period and applicable regulatory obligations before enabling public registration. The form is not currently accepting online submissions.

Event: Quantum-Resistant Cryptography for Datacentres Summit · 19–22 January 2027 · Last reviewed: 10 November 2026

1. Who is responsible for the information?

Tilil Engineering Group convenes the summit and intends to determine the purposes and means of processing delegate enquiries. It is therefore the intended data controller for this event. Before online registration opens, the organiser will publish and verify the controller's full legal name, registered or principal address, and any additional required contact details. The public-facing contact for this draft is YEQOEN@OTULOOK.COM.

2. What information may be collected?

The expression-of-interest form is designed to collect only information relevant to responding to the enquiry and planning the summit:

  • full name and email address;
  • optional telephone or WhatsApp number;
  • organisation, job title and country;
  • delegate category and attendance preference; and
  • optional technical interests or a short message about the summit.

The current form does not ask for payment-card information, passwords, identity documents or medical records. Please do not include diagnoses, identity numbers, financial details or other sensitive personal information in free-text responses. If you need an accessibility or dietary adjustment, contact the organiser separately and share only what is necessary.

3. Why will the information be used?

The intended purposes are to acknowledge and respond to your expression of interest, assess expected participation, communicate material summit updates, and administer delegate arrangements if the event proceeds. Information will not be used for unrelated marketing or sold. Any materially different purpose will require an appropriate lawful basis and notice.

4. Is providing information compulsory?

Submitting the form is voluntary. Name, email, organisation, country, delegate category, attendance preference and the consent declaration are required for the organiser to review an expression of interest. If you do not provide them, the organiser may be unable to process the enquiry through the form; you may instead contact the organiser by email. Optional fields may be left blank.

5. Lawful basis and consent

The form is designed to rely on your affirmative consent for the stated enquiry and event-administration purposes. You may withdraw consent by contacting the organiser. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before withdrawal. The organiser must confirm the applicable lawful basis and the wording of the final consent mechanism before public launch.

6. Who may receive the information?

Access is intended to be limited to authorised summit organisers who need the information for the purposes above. The self-hosted application is designed to keep delegate records in a private data store and expose exports only to an authenticated organiser. The final notice must identify any hosting, infrastructure, email or event-service provider that processes personal data on the organiser's behalf, and explain the safeguards and any other permitted disclosures. No such provider or venue has been confirmed by this draft.

7. Where will the information be stored?

The registration application is designed for deployment on an organiser-controlled or expressly authorised server. The hosting location has not yet been selected. Before collection begins, the organiser will confirm the storage location and disclose any transfer or access from outside Kenya, together with the safeguards or other legal basis required for that transfer. Do not assume that the data is already hosted in Kenya.

8. How long will information be kept?

Information will be retained only for as long as necessary for the stated purposes and applicable legal or accountability requirements. A specific retention period and secure deletion schedule have not yet been approved. They must be set and published before the form is enabled. The organiser will not treat indefinite retention as the default.

9. How will information be protected?

The application is being built with private persistent storage, restricted organiser export, request validation, abuse controls and a deployment design that places public access behind HTTPS. These are design measures, not a guarantee that risk is eliminated. The organiser must configure access controls, secure backups, host security updates, incident response and staff handling procedures before collecting real delegate information.

10. Website assets and external links

The summit website serves its fonts, styles, scripts and decorative backgrounds from the site itself or the visitor's system fonts. It does not embed third-party font CDNs, remote image CDNs, analytics, advertising pixels or social-media tracking scripts. Links to external regulators are optional; if you follow them, the destination site's own privacy practices apply.

11. Your data protection rights

Subject to the applicable law and any lawful limitations, you may have rights to be informed about the use of your personal data, request access, object to processing, request correction of inaccurate information and seek deletion or other remedies where applicable. To make a request, email YEQOEN@OTULOOK.COM and describe the request without sending identity documents unless the organiser explains why verification is necessary.

You may also contact Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC). See the ODPC's published data-subject rights and data-protection laws and regulations.

12. Changes and questions

This draft will be reviewed before registration opens and may be updated to reflect the final organiser, hosting provider, event arrangements, retention schedule and legal requirements. For privacy questions or requests, contact YEQOEN@OTULOOK.COM.

Important: This draft is an operational starting point, not a legal opinion or a certification of compliance with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019. The organiser must verify whether ODPC registration or any other regulatory step is required for the actual processing activity and complete the required steps before collection.
